KCl is less soluble at room temperature than at 80~C. On cooling a solution that was saturated at 80~C, some dissolved KCl therefore crystallises out until the remaining solution reaches its new saturation concentration. At particle level, dissolved particles leave the solution and become arranged in a solid.
