I Properties of Matter

The students…

  • know the definition of the term chemistry.
  • know the GHS pictograms and their meanings.
  • understand why experiments are essential in science.
  • know the basic building blocks of a lab report and know how to write a lab report for an experiment.
  • know the definition of the particle model and are able to apply it to different situations.
  • know what a chemical reaction is, can classify reactions as exothermic or endothermic and can draw the corresponding energy diagrams.
  • can formulate reaction equations using names of substances and understand the process on a particle level.
  • know the definition of the term substance
  • can convert from decimal to scientific to prefix notation and vice versa
  • can convert units
  • understand the material properties melting point and boiling point and know how different factors influence them.
  • know the characteristics of the states of matter and the transitions between them on the macroscopic level (= our daily lives) and on a particle level.
  • understand the property solubility and know the different terms connected with solubility (solute, solvent, dissolved, saturated solution, solubility).
  • understand solubility diagrams and can extract information of a diagram.
  • understand the material property density and know how different factors influence it.
  • are able to solve simple problems concerning density.
  • understand the concept which explains the temperature of a substance.
  • know the term diffusion and osmosis and can explain them by using examples.
  • know and understand the terms homogeneous mixtures and heterogeneous mixtures.
  • know the names of the different types of mixtures and can draw them on a particle level and can name an example.
  • can name and describe a number of important separation methods.
  • understand the principles which lie behind these separation methods also on a particle level.
  • can use the terms pure substance, mixture, element, and compound to explain the division of matter which is commonly used in chemistry.
  • understand all exercises and experiments

Objectives in bold are also relevant for future exams
